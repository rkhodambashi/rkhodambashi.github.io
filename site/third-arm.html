Prosthetic & wearable robotics / 8 videos

Expanding the possibilities of movement.

Two complementary projects at Georgia Tech: a drumming prosthesis for an amputee musician, and a wearable robotic arm to expand musicians' capabilities.

Georgia Institute of Technology / 2014-2016

This portfolio features publicly available demonstrations of my development work. Confidential company information and proprietary design details are excluded.

My engineering contribution

My work followed two paths: helping an amputee drummer control a robotic prosthesis through muscle activity, and developing a wearable robotic arm that gives musicians an additional limb.

For the prosthetic project, I worked directly with Jason on EMG electrode placement and signal acquisition, and tested mapping my own muscle signals to movements of the drumming arm. The work also extended to a humanoid hand.

For wearable augmentation, I designed and prototyped the arm, developed SOLIDWORKS models, implemented input shaping control, and integrated sensors for motion control. The demonstrations trace its development and related applications in musical performance.
