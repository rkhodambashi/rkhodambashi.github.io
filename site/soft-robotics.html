Hydrogel soft robotics / 6 videos

Small robots. Responsive materials.

Temperature-responsive hydrogel actuators explored through untethered robots, miniature mechanisms, and a soft robotic manipulator.

Arizona State University / 2017-2021

My engineering contribution

During my doctoral research, I formulated and validated stimuli-responsive hydrogel actuators, using thermal and structural analysis to improve actuator response. These demonstrations show the range of miniature robotic systems investigated with hydrogel actuation.
