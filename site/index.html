03Manufacturing automation

Calviri · 2022–2024

Assay process development & automation

System design across chip fabrication, packaging, and assay equipment, reconciling interdependent requirements to enable automation throughout the workflow.

90%equipment cost reduction through a reconfigurable mask subsystem
Engineering contribution +

Developed processes and hardware across fabrication, chip packaging, and assay automation, bringing requirements from manufacturing and downstream equipment into the design. Packaging served as the interface between the chips, robotic handling, and assay instruments.

Designed, manufactured, and validated an automated synthesis system. Replaced fixed glass masks with a reconfigurable photolithography mask subsystem, enabling three times higher iteration efficiency.

Collaborated on wafer layout and chip sizing to improve production yield by 30%, and engineered a pick-and-place robot for silicon chip packaging.

Process development / System design / Automation integration

04Precision mechatronics

Kinematics · 2024–present

Satellite Tracking and Motion Control

Precision mechanisms and control systems for satellite tracking, combining mechanical design, sensing, and trajectory generation.

0.03°actuator backlash, reduced from 0.2°
Engineering contribution +

Designed a two-degree-of-freedom XY positioner with planetary and worm gear trains, applying GD&T and tolerance analysis for sub-degree alignment.

Integrated BLDC drives and absolute rotary encoders, simulated robotic systems in ROS 2 and Gazebo, and developed satellite trajectory generation and tracking algorithms in Python.

Mechanical design / Motion control / ROS 2 / Python
